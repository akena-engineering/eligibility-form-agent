# Sources

The form the agent fills, `forms/arkansas-wioa-2.4.pdf`, is a real public form, unchanged:
https://dws.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/FORM_2.4_-_Dislocated_Worker_Eligibility_Determination_6-24-21_-1.pdf

The case documents in `cases/` are real public forms filled with synthetic data:

- Arkansas Division of Workforce Services, FORM WIOA I-B – 1.1 Application, Adult & Dislocated
  Worker Programs (updated 8/27/18):
  https://dws.arkansas.gov/wp-content/uploads/FORM-1.1-Application-Adult-DLW-Updated-8-27-18.pdf
- IRS Schedule C (Form 1040) 2025, Profit or Loss From Business:
  https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040sc.pdf

The unemployment insurance monetary determination in Anna's case has no Arkansas sample. Its
structure follows New York's public notice T402B (https://forms.labor.ny.gov/UI/T402B.pdf): claim
summary, base-period wages by employer and quarter, weekly benefit calculation. The wording is ours.

Everything written into these forms is synthetic: invented people, employers, numbers and dates.
